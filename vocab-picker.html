How to create AI flashcards

Follow these steps to turn any paragraph into smart, AI-generated Anki flashcards using Vocab Picker, Claude, and AnkiMCP.

1

Find a paragraph & paste it in

Pick any reading passage — an article, textbook excerpt, IELTS practice text, anything. Paste it into the text area above, or click "Load passage" to fetch one automatically from naughmilkielts.com. Each word becomes clickable.

↗ Browse all reading tests ↗ Try Passage: The Impact of the Potato
2

Click the words you want to remember

Click each word you don't know or want to memorize. Selected words turn orange and appear in the "Noted Vocabulary" box below. Take your time — only pick the words that matter to you.

When you're done, click Export as .txt to download your word list.

3

Open Claude and load your word list

Open Claude.ai and create a new conversation. Paste your exported word list (the .txt file contents).

Then copy and paste the prompt below into Claude:

Prompt for Claude
Here are some vocabulary words I want to learn: [PASTE YOUR EXPORTED WORDS HERE] For each word, create an Anki flashcard using AnkiMCP. Use the create_note tool to add each card to a deck called "Vocabulary". Format each card with: - Front: the vocabulary word - Back: pronunciation (if applicable), English definition, and an example sentence using the word in context
↗ Open Claude.ai ↗ Learn about AnkiMCP
4

Let Claude & AnkiMCP generate your cards

Claude will use AnkiMCP to create flashcard notes directly in Anki. You'll see the cards appear in your Anki deck called "Vocabulary" — no manual card creation needed.

↗ Download Anki ↗ Open AnkiWeb
5

Study in Anki

Open Anki on your device, select the "Vocabulary" deck, and start studying. Anki's spaced repetition algorithm will show you each card at the optimal time to maximize long-term retention.

↗ Download Anki ↗ AnkiWeb sync
Pro tip: For the best results, paste the full paragraph along with your word list into Claude. This gives Claude context to write more accurate, sentence-based definitions that match the passage's meaning.
